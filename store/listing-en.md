# LinkedDraw for LinkedIn

## Short description

Draw directly on your LinkedIn profile cover, then finish through LinkedIn's normal upload dialog.

## Full description

LinkedDraw adds a compact drawing editor beside the cover on your own LinkedIn profile.

Use a pen, line, arrow, rectangle, text, or eraser; choose colors and sizes; undo, redo, clear, and optionally show guides for the profile photo and cover controls. The final image is exported at 1584×396 as PNG or JPEG and handed to LinkedIn's visible upload flow.

LinkedDraw is local-first:

- no account with the extension developer;
- no analytics, advertising, telemetry, or remote code;
- no reading of LinkedIn cookies, messages, contacts, or access tokens;
- no private LinkedIn API;
- settings stay in browser extension storage;
- generated drawings remain in memory until you save.

If LinkedIn's interface changes and automatic handoff cannot complete safely, LinkedDraw downloads the generated image so your work is not lost.

English and Ukrainian interfaces are included.

LinkedDraw is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.
